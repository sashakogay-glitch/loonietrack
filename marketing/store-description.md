## Short description (80 chars max)

## Full description

Built in Canada, for Canadians.

LoonieTrack turns a photo of a receipt into a tax-ready expense in seconds.
Point your camera at the receipt — the app reads the merchant, the amount and
the HST, picks the right category, and files it away. No shoebox in April.

WHAT IT DOES

- Smart receipt scanner — snap a photo, the app extracts amount, tax and merchant
- Automatic HST/GST/PST by province — Ontario, Alberta, Quebec and the rest, each with the correct rate
- Personal and corporate expenses kept apart — one app, two sets of books
- One-touch tax reports — T1 for sole proprietors, T2 for incorporated businesses
- HST ITC tracking and quarterly return summaries
- Works offline — record an expense in a dead zone, it syncs when you are back online
- Export to PDF for your accountant

WHO IT IS FOR

Self-employed Canadians and owner-operators: truckers, contractors, tradespeople,
consultants, delivery drivers — anyone who pays their own taxes and would rather
spend the evening at home than sorting paper.

WHY CANADIAN-BUILT MATTERS

Most expense trackers were made for somewhere else. They do not know what an HST
input tax credit is, they cannot tell Ontario from Alberta, and they have never
heard of a T2. LoonieTrack was built here, for the way Canadians actually file.

FREE AND PRO

Free: 10 receipts a month, personal expenses, basic categories.
Pro Personal: unlimited receipts, full T1 reports, export for your accountant.
Pro Business: everything above, plus corporate expenses, T2 reports and HST ITC tracking.

Reference only — consult a CPA before filing with CRA.
