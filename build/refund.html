Refund Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This Refund Policy applies to subscriptions purchased for LoonieTrack (Personal and Business plans) through our payments provider, Paddle.com. Paddle acts as the Merchant of Record for all purchases, and payments are processed by Paddle.

1. Free plan

LoonieTrack offers a free plan with no payment required. You can use the free plan indefinitely with no obligation to upgrade.

2. Monthly subscriptions

  • Personal and Business subscriptions are billed monthly and renew automatically until cancelled.
  • You can cancel anytime from within the app (Profile → Manage Subscription). Your paid features remain active until the end of the billing period you already paid for — we do not refund the unused portion of a cancelled month, and access is not cut off immediately upon cancellation.
  • If you cancel, your account automatically moves to the Free plan at the end of the period. Your data, transaction history, and receipts are never deleted when you downgrade.

3. Requesting a refund

Monthly subscription charges are generally non-refundable once the billing period has started, since access to Pro features is granted immediately upon payment. However, we want you to be satisfied — if you believe you were charged in error, were double-charged, or have another legitimate concern, please contact us within 14 days of the charge:

  • Email hello@loonietrack.ca with your account email and the reason for your request.
  • We will review each request individually and respond within 5 business days.

Refunds, when approved, are issued to the original payment method via Paddle and may take 5–10 business days to appear, depending on your bank or card issuer.

4. Failed or disputed payments

If a renewal payment fails (for example, an expired card), your account is automatically moved to the Free plan — your data is preserved and no further charges are made. If your bank disputes or reverses a charge (chargeback), your account will be downgraded to the Free plan while the dispute is resolved.

5. Contact

Questions about billing or refunds? Email hello@loonietrack.ca.
